# PRODUCT.md

## One line

Short, animated reels that re-teach the KTU B.Tech CSE syllabus topic by topic, in everyday Malayalam–English, so struggling students can catch up and clear their papers.

## Problem

Students moving from school to engineering college enter a new world with many distractions and little structure. Study slips, lectures go over their heads, and failed exams pile up as back papers across S1–S8. Textbooks are dense and in formal English; recorded lectures are long. There is little content that is short, clear, visual, in the language they think in, and mapped exactly to their syllabus.

## Who it's for

- KTU B.Tech CSE students (2024 scheme first), especially those who find classes hard to follow
- Students preparing for internal and end-semester exams, or clearing back papers
- Later: other branches, other schemes, and autonomous colleges with their own curricula

## What a reel is

- About 60 seconds, one topic, one idea
- Vertical video (1080×1920), watchable on a phone, captions always on
- Malayalam narration with English technical terms kept in English
- Always includes a concrete example a Kerala student recognises
- Structure: hook → explain → example → show (diagram, code, steps or table) → takeaway
- Labelled with semester, subject and module, so a student knows exactly where it fits

## How the content is organised

Semester → Subject → Module → Topic (one reel). The syllabus is the map: a student facing a Module 3 question finds every Module 3 topic in order. Topics are marked Major (core concepts and likely exam questions) or Minor.

## Scope

In now:

- 17 core subjects, S1–S6, KTU 2024 CSE scheme (536 reels)
- Reel production pipeline: script → review → voice → render → publish
- Student web app: browse by semester/subject/module, player, short quiz per reel, progress

Later:

- Module recap videos and longer worked-problem videos for 9-mark questions
- Electives (only those students actually take)
- Other schemes (2019), other curricula (autonomous colleges), other branches
- Accounts, synced progress, analytics

Not planned:

- Replacing textbooks or lectures; reels are for understanding and revision
- AI avatars or stock footage

## Student flow

1. Open the app, pick semester and subject.
2. See modules, each with its reels in order; Major reels marked.
3. Watch a reel; answer three quick questions; move to the next reel.
4. See progress per module.

## Content principles

- Accuracy first. Every reel is reviewed by a subject expert before it is voiced.
- One idea per reel. If it needs "also", split it.
- Every reel has an example from life in Kerala: a college canteen, KSRTC routes, a hospital queue, a ration shop register, an exam hall seating plan.
- Explain the idea in plain words, then name the term.
- Original writing only. Never copy a textbook's or another creator's wording.

## Design principles

- Readable on a small phone at arm's length
- Motion explains, never decorates
- Calm pacing: nothing appears before the narration mentions it
- One consistent look ("backwater dusk") so every reel feels part of one series

## Success criteria

- Pilot (Programming in C): students who watch a module's reels score better on that module's quiz than before, and finish most reels they start
- A module of about 9 reels goes from plan to published in half a day of human time
- Reviewers find no factual error in a published reel
